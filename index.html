Arithmetic Helpers

Type in a problem and see every step written out, the way it's done on paper, with tape diagrams and area models alongside. Works with whole numbers, decimals and fractions.

+

The Carry Column

Addition: line up the decimal points or find a common denominator, and watch every carry.

 −

The Borrow Bank

Subtraction: every borrow crossed out and written above, so you can see where the ten came from.

 ×

The Product Ladder

Multiplication: one rung per digit, partial products stacked and added, then the decimal point.

 ÷

The Division House

Long division: divide, multiply, subtract, bring down, with remainders, decimals or fractions.
